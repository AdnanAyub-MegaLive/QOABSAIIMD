Room Management: All rooms · Bans and kicks

GET /api/admin/room-management/moderation requires rooms.view.
Filters: type=ALL|KICK|BAN|SEAT_KICK; status=ALL|ACTIVE|EXPIRED|REVOKED;
q searches target name/public ID and room title/ID. from/to use UTC, with a
date-only to including the entire day. page starts at 1; 50 rows per page,
hasMore indicates another page. Kicks and unbans have status COMPLETED and
appear under status ALL. Ban rows use authoritative ban state, including old
expired and revoked records. No historical backfill or deletion is performed.

DELETE /api/admin/room-management?banId=RBAN-... requires rooms.manage.
Revocation, ROOM_UNBANNED log with Portal Admin attribution, and audit are
atomic. Retrying an already-revoked ban does not create duplicate logs.
Other restrictions (account bans, blocks, paid/private access) still apply.

audio-room:seat-kick accepts optional reason (300 characters). The seat update
and SEAT_KICK moderation log commit together. Existing canManageSeats checks
remain unchanged. No VIP benefit settings are added, removed or broadened.

Animated, interactive prototype

Your flight, your exit

1. Choose your coins and get on board before launch.

2. The payout multiplier starts at 0× and climbs toward 5×. Cashing out below 1× returns less than you boarded with.

3. Tap Cash out to lock in the displayed return. If the rocket crashes first, your boarded coins are lost.

The rocket crashes automatically at a randomly selected level from 0× through 5×, including an immediate crash at 0×. Cash out before it crashes. A successful cash-out stays safe even if the rocket later crashes.

This is a design demo using practice coins. The live wallet, shared room clock, crash probabilities and server-confirmed cash-outs are not connected.

Reseller business/personal wallet separation

Status: implemented locally; not deployed. Apply migration
20261022010000_reseller_business_wallet before starting the new code.
Existing coinBalance values and ledger history remain unchanged. New
resellerCoinBalance starts at zero for all accounts. No historical allocation.

Admin: open a reseller's User profile, then Manage reseller business wallet.
Direct page: /reseller-wallet?userId=USR-...
Requires finance.adjust. Funding accepts 1–500,000,000 Coins and a reason,
with an idempotency key generated by the form, ledger entry and audit record.
Existing generic admin Coin-add action refuses reseller funding and directs
admins to this separate business wallet control. Personal deductions remain
personal adjustments. No business-to-personal automatic migration exists.

GET /api/wallet (existing v1 alias):
coins and personalCoins = personal spendable Coins (decimal strings).
businessCoins = separate business balance (decimal string).
coinRecharge describes BUSINESS -> PERSONAL, selfRechargeAllowed,
reverseTransferAllowed:false and idempotencyKeyRequired:true.
Existing canTransferCoins and role/account restrictions remain authoritative.

POST /api/wallet/transfers (existing v1 alias):
Authorization: Bearer <session>
Idempotency-Key: client-generated stable unique ID (8–128 chars)
{ "recipientPublicId":"USR-...", "coins":"1000" }
Alternatively send requestId in JSON. Same request key must be reused after
network failure. A changed recipient/amount with that key is rejected.
Using the reseller's own public ID is supported and recharges PERSONAL Coins.
All other recipients also receive PERSONAL Coins, even if they are resellers.
Never use personal Coins to fund a business recharge. No reverse endpoint.
Limits still use WALLET_TRANSFER_MIN_COINS and WALLET_TRANSFER_MAX_COINS.

Success data: transactionId, referenceId, remainingCoins (= BUSINESS balance),
businessCoins, personalCoins, sourceWallet:"BUSINESS",
destinationWallet:"PERSONAL". Replay returns the original result, including
the original balance snapshot; fetch wallet for today's balance.

Ledger DTO adds wallet:"BUSINESS" or "PERSONAL". Historical entries without
wallet metadata are PERSONAL. A self-recharge records two linked entries:
business debit and personal credit with the same referenceId. Business
recharges do not increment personal totalSpent or grant gift XP.

Reseller Diamond redemption still requires admin approval. Approval now
credits only the business wallet and labels its ledger entry BUSINESS.
Pending requests approved after deployment use the business wallet. Previously
completed requests are not moved or credited again. Status/role restrictions,
including CS/CS Leader precedence, are rechecked on approval.

Personal purchases, ordinary rewards, existing payment-provider personal
top-ups and gift spending remain on coinBalance. They do not fund business.

QA: admin funds business without changing personal balance; self-recharge
debits business and credits personal equally; customer recharge leaves sender
personal balance unchanged; insufficient business funds fail even when
personal funds suffice; same-key retries do not duplicate; revoked/CS roles
fail; approved redemption credits business only; no historical balances move.
Production and real-device verification remain pending. Deploy mobile changes

Account and data deletion requests

You can request deletion of your Mega Live account and associated personal data, or request deletion of specific data without closing your account. You do not need to sign in to this webpage.

Send your request

  1. Email support@megalive.live with the subject Mega Live account deletion request or Mega Live data deletion request.
  2. Include your Mega Live user ID and tell us whether you want to delete your account or specific data, such as your profile photo, posts or messages. If possible, send the request from the email associated with your account.
  3. Support will need to verify that you own the account before taking action. Do not send your password, verification codes, bank details or identity documents in your initial email.

Email a deletion request

If your device does not have an email app, copy the address above into your email service. Opening the email link does not submit a request; you must send the email.

Processing time

Our support team monitors support@megalive.live and processes deletion requests within a maximum of five business days. If we need information to verify account ownership, we will contact you. We will confirm the outcome by email and explain any data that cannot be deleted and why.

What we delete

After verifying your request, we disable sign-in and revoke sessions, then remove your profile and contact details, login identifiers, uploaded profile and cover media, your posts and comments, your messages and uploaded chat media, device records, social connections, CP history, task and game history, and personal inventory. Rooms you own are closed and their personal media is removed. Copies of app-hosted media are removed from storage and invalidated on our CDN.

Balances and pending transactions

Deletion closes the account and removes its personal wallet balances and inventory. Contact support about withdrawals, disputes or pending payments before requesting deletion. Outstanding settlements must be reconciled before cleanup can finish; support will explain any delay. Deleting an account does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription or request a purchase refund. Manage subscriptions and refunds through Google Play.

Records that remain

We keep de-identified transaction amounts and settlement references for accounting, fraud prevention and protection of other users' financial records. These records no longer contain your profile, email, phone number or login credentials. Other users' own messages and independently saved copies are not deleted from their devices. We review requests concerning data held by our service providers and request removal where applicable.

Restricted technical/security logs expire through rotation within 30 days. They are used for reliability and abuse investigation, not to restore your profile.

Backup retention

Restricted database backups expire within 30 days of creation. They are not used for normal app access. If a backup is restored, deletion recovery checks prevent deleted accounts from being reopened until their deletion is reapplied. Minimal recovery identifiers are kept until backups that could contain the account have expired, then removed.

Delete specific data

You can delete your own posts and supported profile media in the app, or email support to request removal of specific data without closing your account. Support verifies ownership and reviews the scope before acting. We will tell you if a record must be retained and why.

You can also find these instructions inside Mega Live under Settings → Help Center, in the account deletion guide. Newer versions provide a direct “Delete account and data” link in Settings.

Sending a request does not confirm completion. Support will confirm the outcome after verification and cleanup. Updated 15 November 2026.

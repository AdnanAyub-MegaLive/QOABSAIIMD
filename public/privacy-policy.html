Privacy Policy

Updated 15 November 2026. This policy describes how Mega Live processes information when you use our app, rooms, games, wallet and support services. Contact support@megalive.live with privacy questions or requests.

Information we process

  • Account and profile information: user identifiers, display name, email or phone where supplied, Google sign-in identifiers, profile images, country, birthday, gender and biography.
  • Content you provide: posts, photos, videos, messages, voice messages, live audio/video, room content and reports.
  • Wallet and purchase information: coin and diamond transactions, gifts, purchases and Google Play purchase references. Where you request payouts or verification, we process the payment details and identity information you provide.
  • App use and device information: interactions, searches, room activity, game activity, device identifiers, connection information, diagnostic and crash information.
  • Location: country or approximate location derived from your connection, and precise location when you grant permission and use a location feature. Coordinates may be sent to the reverse-geocoding provider to identify your location.

Why we use it

We use this information to authenticate accounts, provide social and live-room features, display profiles and rankings, deliver messages, process purchases and rewards, manage wallets, respond to support requests, prevent fraud and abuse, and maintain and improve app reliability.

Who receives information

Your public profile, posts, room participation and relevant rankings are visible to other users. Messages are delivered to their intended participants. Service providers process information to provide hosting, media storage and delivery, live audio/video, authentication, payments and technical operations. These include DigitalOcean, our configured live-communication provider, and Google for Google sign-in and Play purchases. Location lookup sends coordinates to the geocoding provider when that feature is used. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or needed to investigate fraud or protect users.

We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Security

We use encrypted network connections, access controls and restricted credentials to protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security. Private messages are not represented as end-to-end encrypted; authorized service processing is required to deliver and operate messaging.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, restrict supported profile visibility, block or report users, and control camera, microphone, photo and location permissions through your device settings. Optional permissions are needed only for their corresponding features. You can contact support to request access, correction or deletion of your information.

Account deletion and retention

Request deletion inside Settings → Help Center or through our account and data deletion page. Email support with your user ID; do not send passwords or verification codes. We verify ownership and aim to process requests within five business days. Unresolved payments or disputes may require reconciliation, and we will explain any delay.

Account deletion removes personal identity, credentials, owned content and associated activity from active systems and removes app-hosted personal media. We retain de-identified transaction amounts and settlement references for accounting and fraud prevention, without profile or contact details. Restricted database backups expire within 30 days of creation; recovery identifiers are retained only until backups that could restore the account have expired. Provider-held copies are reviewed as part of deletion. Copies independently saved by other users are outside our direct control.

Restricted technical/security logs expire through rotation within 30 days and are used for reliability and abuse investigation.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as our service changes. The date above identifies the latest version.

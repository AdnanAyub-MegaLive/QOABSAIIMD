# Greedy reference artwork (v2)

Generated with the built-in image generation tool using the supplied Greedy video frame as a style reference.

Prompt: Create six isolated transparent cartoon sprites in a 3-by-2 grid: a wooden food stall with a blank number sign and three paw-print windows; a cheerful lion cub with white headband in a golden frame and blank stopwatch; salad; pizza; a blank coin-selection plaque; and parchment with pencil. Match the warm rendered carnival style. No numbers or labels baked in.

Follow-up prompt: Remove only the blurred background, preserve all six objects and their positions, and output transparent alpha.

Extracted into stall-v2.webp, mascot-v2.webp, salad-v2.webp, pizza-v2.webp, plaque-v2.webp and history-v2.webp. Dynamic amounts, timing and multipliers remain rendered by the UI.
